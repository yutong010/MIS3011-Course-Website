Advanced AI for Business: From Theories to Applications

MIS3011 | CUHKSZ | Fall 2026

Course Overview

Teach Less,Learn More Slide Less,Build More

English

This course builds a comprehensive understanding of the AI landscape for business students, from classic techniques to cutting-edge technologies, with a consistent focus on business application value. The goal is not just to teach technical skills, but to empower you to become AI-native talent, using AI as a core driver for workflow design and problem-solving.

Key topics: (1) how to identify business problems that AI can solve, scope AI projects effectively, and recognize the problems AI should not be used for; (2) modern AI technologies, such as decision AI, neural networks, language models, prompt engineering, retrieval-augmented generation, agentic workflow, agent engineering, vision AI, transfer learning, and multimodal AI; (3) how to verify AI models and AI systems actually work; (4) how to evaluate trade-offs across accuracy, cost, latency, and deployment in real-world settings.

The course focuses on BREADTH, positioning you as an AI General Practitioner who can diagnose a wide range of business problems and apply the right technique from a versatile toolkit. In-class labs, individual assignments, and a group project put every one of these judgments into practice.

主要内容：（1）如何识别哪些商业问题可以用 AI 解决、如何界定 AI 项目的范围，以及哪些问题不该交给 AI；（2）现代 AI 技术，包括决策式 AI、神经网络、语言模型、提示工程、检索增强生成、agentic workflow、agent 工程、视觉 AI、迁移学习与多模态 AI；（3）如何验证 AI 模型与 AI 系统真的可用；（4）如何在准确率、成本、延迟与部署之间做权衡。

本课程以广度为主，把你培养成 AI"全科医生"：面对各类商业问题，能判断该用哪一类 AI 技术。课堂 lab、个人作业与小组项目把上述判断落到真实的商业问题上。

Learning Outcomes

By the end of the course you will be able to design, implement, and evaluate AI solutions across a range of business settings. The learning objectives group into five capabilities:

Capability 1
Explain and Anticipate
  • Clearly explain what an AI model is actually optimizing
  • Anticipate how a model will fail when data, users, or business conditions change
Capability 2
Scope and Select
  • Assess which business problems warrant an AI solution and which do not
  • Select the appropriate AI techniques for a given business problem
Capability 3
Build and Deliver
  • Implement AI models through code-based development (Python, Colab)
  • Build AI solutions using low-code and no-code platforms and coding agents (Dify, Coze, n8n, Codex, WorkBuddy)
Capability 4
Verify and Take Responsibility
  • Rigorously evaluate an AI system against eval datasets
  • Identify the ethical risks of an AI deployment
Capability 5
Communicate and Collaborate
  • Professionally explain technical AI work to both technical professionals and non-technical stakeholders
  • Effectively direct AI agents and coding assistants

Assessment Scheme

In-Class Participation (6%)
  • A question asked or answered earns 1 point
  • A demo or presentation of your own work earns 2 points
  • SIX points is the maximum
  • Make sure the TA records your name
In-Class Lab Submission (9%)
  • Most sessions end with a lab you complete in class
  • Submit the required artefact to Blackboard by the end of that day
  • 1.5 points per accepted submission, up to NINE

Note: There are no sign-ins and no attendance marks. Sending emails, talking to me during breaks or outside the classroom, and attending Office Hours are always welcome, but they do NOT count toward Participation.

TWO Assignments, 10% each
  • Each assignment asks you to build something and to evaluate whether it works, using Python/Colab, no-code platforms, and coding agents. Both are individual work.
  • Assignment Awards: for each assignment the teaching team selects a limited number of submissions for a title (Best Interface Designer, Best Evaluator, Best Developer and others) and a small prize. Awards carry no marks.
  • Late Submission Policy: you have one "late submission" for the semester, allowing one assignment to be submitted up to 48 hours late without penalty. You must inform the TA when using it.
  • After the late submission is used, a penalty of 20% per day will be applied.

Exam Format
  • Closed-book
  • Paper-based
  • You will not be asked to write code
