# MIS3011 - Advanced AI for Business: From Theories to Applications

## Course Website - Fall 2026

This repository contains the course website for MIS3011 at CUHK(SZ), designed to provide students with comprehensive information about the course structure, learning outcomes, schedule, and resources.

### Course Highlights

The course focuses on BREADTH, positioning students as AI General Practitioners. Five capabilities are developed across the term:

1. **Explain and Anticipate** - what a model is actually optimizing, and how it fails when data, users, or business conditions change
2. **Scope and Select** - which business problems warrant an AI solution, and which technique fits
3. **Build and Deliver** - Python/Colab, low-code and no-code platforms, and coding agents
4. **Verify and Take Responsibility** - evaluation against eval datasets, and the ethical risks of a deployment
5. **Communicate and Collaborate** - explaining technical work to any audience, and directing AI agents

Students gain hands-on experience through in-class labs, individual assignments, and a group project built around real business problems.
